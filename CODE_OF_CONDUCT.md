# Code of Conduct

`autonomyx` is an open, community project. We are committed to a welcoming,
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Our standards

- Be respectful, considerate, and collaborative.
- Welcome newcomers; assume good faith.
- Critique ideas, not people. Disagree technically, in the open.
- No harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.

## Adoption

This project adopts the **CNCF Community Code of Conduct**
(https://github.com/cncf/foundation/blob/main/code-of-conduct.md), itself based
on the **Contributor Covenant**. As the project pursues CNCF Sandbox, this is the
standard we hold ourselves to from the start.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers via a private channel (to be
listed in `MAINTAINERS` as the project grows). Reports are handled
confidentially and in good faith.
