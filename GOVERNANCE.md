# Governance

`autonomyx` (the box) is **open governance, not a walled garden.** This is the
keystone of the project: the contract and meaning are a **commons**, operated in
the open. The commercial **platform** is a separate concern and does not govern
the box.

## Principles

- **Open by default.** Decisions, design, and discussion happen in public.
- **Conformance over control.** What "a real box" means is defined by a public
  spec and tests anyone can meet — not by gatekeeping.
- **Compose, don't fork-and-impersonate.** Forking is welcome (under your own
  name); claiming to be the canonical box without conforming is not.

## Roles

- **Contributors** — anyone who opens an issue or PR. (Sign off with DCO.)
- **Maintainers** — review and merge, steward the roadmap, uphold the spec and
  the Code of Conduct. Listed in `MAINTAINERS` (to be added as the project grows).

## Becoming a maintainer

Sustained, high-quality contributions + review participation. Existing
maintainers nominate; decision by lazy consensus (see below).

## Decisions

**Lazy consensus.** A proposal (PR/issue) with no sustained objection after a
reasonable review window is accepted. Significant or contested changes are
resolved by maintainer consensus, in public. We document non-trivial design
decisions (ADRs) so the *why* is part of the record — provenance for choices.

## Direction: open foundation

We intend to grow `autonomyx` **collaboratively with the community** and to
pursue **CNCF Sandbox** as the project matures (open license, open governance,
clear cloud-native scope, demonstrable adoption). This document is a starting
point and will evolve toward a CNCF-aligned governance model.
